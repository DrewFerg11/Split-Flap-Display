## AC mains voltage

Option A of the dual display build involves **hardwiring to AC mains voltage (110/240V)**. Mains voltage is lethal. If you are not comfortable working with mains wiring, **do not attempt Option A** — use Option B (external barrel jack adapter) or consult a qualified electrician.
